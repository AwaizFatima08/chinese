Lingo15: Chinese & German

Android app by HomiLabs · package com.homilabs.lingo15

Learn Chinese (HSK 1–6) and German (A1–C2) in short daily lessons. Listen, speak, write and review in 15 minutes a day. The app works without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and only backs up your progress so you can continue on another device.

Quick answers

  • Do I need an account? No. Everything works offline and without signing in.
  • What does signing in do? It stores a backup of your progress under your Google account so you can restore it on a new phone or use two devices.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings → Backup and sync → Delete account and cloud data. Or follow the email steps.
  • Ads? The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads limited to family-suitable content.
