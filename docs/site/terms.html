Beam: Torch, Strobe & SOS

Additional terms for Beam · updated 27 September 2026

Flashing lights

Beam's strobe, SOS and Morse features flash light rapidly, on the camera flash or on the screen. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Don't use these features if you or people near you may be affected, and stop straight away if anyone feels unwell.

Use the light responsibly

  • Never shine the torch or a strobe into anyone's eyes.
  • Never point it at drivers, pilots, aircraft, vessels or animals, or use it where it could distract or dazzle someone.
  • The flash and screen get warm and use battery quickly when left on for a long time.

Not an emergency service

The SOS and Morse features are a visual aid only. Beam does not contact anyone, and there is no guarantee that a signal will be seen or understood. In an emergency, always contact your local emergency services by phone first if you can.

Device differences

Adjustable torch brightness needs Android 13 or newer and a flash that supports it. Flash speed and brightness vary between phones.

System Pulse: Net Speed Test

Additional terms for System Pulse · updated 8 October 2026

Measurements are estimates

Speed, latency and quality scores are measured from your phone to Cloudflare's network at that moment and depend on your device, Wi-Fi, time of day and network conditions. They are indicative, not a guaranteed measure of your plan or a basis for any claim against a provider.

Data usage

Each test transfers data in both directions – typically tens of megabytes and more on fast connections. On a metered mobile plan this counts against your allowance. Run tests on Wi-Fi if data is limited.

Bottleneck diagnosis

The Wi-Fi vs. ISP verdict is an automated interpretation of the measurements and can be wrong, for example when a router blocks pings or another device is using the connection. Treat it as a pointer, not a diagnosis.

Coverage map

Map data is contributed anonymously by users and is not verified. It may be sparse or out of date in your area.
